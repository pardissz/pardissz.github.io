▸ EMNLP 2026
EMNLP 2026 Main Conference · Oral Presentation

The Alignment Veto: How Safety Training Suppresses Cultural Knowledge in LLMs

Pardis Sadat Zahraei, Gokhan Tur, Dilek Hakkani-Tür, Ehsaneddin Asgari

Safety training doesn't erase cultural knowledge — it hides it. At refusal, a model's internal logits often match 1.53M human survey responses better than what it actually says.

▸ COLM 2026
🏆 Outstanding Paper · COLM 2026 & ACL 2026 KnowFM Workshop

Emergent Unfaithfulness: How Alignment Training Causes Language Models to Silently Override Task Faithfulness

Pardis Sadat Zahraei, Janvijay Singh, Gokhan Tur, Dilek Hakkani-Tür

Alignment training makes models silently override instructions — and the better the model, the worse it gets. A reverse scaling law.

Asymmetrically Unfair Decision Making and Reasoning in LLMs

Pardis Sadat Zahraei, Faeze Brahman, Dilek Hakkani-Tür, Gokhan Tur, Maarten Sap
